## <mark>AI Generated Content</mark>

<mark>We welcome high quality PRs, whether they are human generated or made with the assistance of AI</mark>
tools, but we ask that you follow these guidelines:

- <mark>**Attribution**: Tell us about your use of AI tools, don't make us guess whether you're using it.</mark>
- <mark>**Review**: Make sure you review every line of AI generated content for correctness and relevance.</mark>
- <mark>**Quality**: AI-generated content should meet the same quality standards as human-written content.</mark>
- <mark>**Quantity**: Avoid submitting large amounts of AI generated content in a single PR. Remember that</mark>
  quality is usually more important than quantity.
- <mark>**License**: Ensure that the AI-generated content is compatible with Ratatui's</mark>
  [License](./LICENSE).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> <mark>Remember that AI tools can assist in generating content, but you are responsible for the final</mark>
> quality and accuracy of the contributions and communicating about it.
