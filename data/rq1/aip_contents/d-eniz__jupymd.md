### <mark>Code quality & "vibe coding"</mark>

If you don't have experience with the Obsidian API, we ask you to refrain from opening pull requests. Your contributions through discussions in issues are just as valuable as pull requests. 

<mark>Asking maintainers to spend time reviewing "vibe coded" pull requests which you haven't spent time on yourself is unacceptable. Current AI tools lack context on the [Obsidian API](https://docs.obsidian.md/Home) and the [Obsidian plugin guidelines](https://docs.obsidian.md/Plugins/Releasing/Plugin+guidelines), and produces unreliable code.</mark>
