## <mark>🤖 Tooling and AI Assistance</mark>

<mark>We welcome and encourage the use of AI tools to support development, but contributors remain responsible for the changes they submit.</mark>

- Make sure you understand every line of code and can explain the design and trade‑offs.
- All code changes must be understood and reviewed by humans.
- <mark>Maintainers reserve the right to close low-context, unexplained, AI-generated PRs without detailed review.</mark>

<mark>For broader project context, see the [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.MD) file.</mark>
