## AI policy

Although Swarm is a game about robots, it is a human-centered project:
the goals for both playing and contributing to Swarm are to have fun,
make friends, and challenge yourself to learn more about programming
and problem solving.  Using AI tools tends to only detract from these
goals.

Therefore, contributions to Swarm may **not** include any content
generated by large language models or other generative AI tools.
Contributions which may not contain AI-generated content include—but
are not limited to—code, comments, documentation, pull request
descriptions, or issues.
