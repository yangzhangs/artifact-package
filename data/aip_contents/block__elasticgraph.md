## <mark>Using AI Tools</mark>

<mark>Using AI tools such as [Goose](https://goose-docs.ai/), [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/), [Cursor](https://cursor.com/),</mark>
<mark>or [Claude](https://claude.ai/) to contribute is encouraged. However:</mark>

* <mark>AI tools are assistants and should not replace critical thinking and judgement.</mark>
* We expect contributors to understand every line of code submitted in a PR--in the long run, humans are on the hook for maintaining it!

<mark>If you use an AI agent, feel free to leverage the growing [AI memory bank](https://github.com/block/elasticgraph/tree/main/ai-memory),</mark>
and if updates made by your AI agent to that directory seem worth keeping, please include them in your submitted PR!

<mark>Primary agent instructions are maintained in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md). We keep [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) as a compatibility symlink.</mark>
