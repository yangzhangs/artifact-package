## <mark>Use of AI</mark>

<mark>We welcome use of AI tools to help draft discussions, issues, or code, but please follow these rules:</mark>

- <mark>Use AI tools responsibly and disclose their use.</mark>
- <mark>Ensure all content passes a human review for authenticity and quality.</mark>
- Be concise. Do not write verbose discussions, issues or PR.

Discussions, issues or PR that consist solely of unvetted AI outputs may be closed at the maintainer's discretion.
