# <mark>LLMs</mark>

<mark>LLM-assisted contributions are welcome, but they must follow our "PR Structure"</mark>
guidelines above, be well-motivated and comprehensible to reviewers, and be
well-understood by the submitter, who must be able to iterate on the PR in
response to review comments just like any other PR. We enforce the [LLVM
<mark>AI Tool Use Policy](./docs/AIToolPolicy.md) which elaborates on this policy.</mark>
Please read that document in full.

Comments, PR descriptions and git commit messages may not be written in full
<mark>by LLMs, unless they are very brief. If maintainers believe they are conversing</mark>
<mark>with a bot and/or being innundated with slop, they may close PRs or issues with</mark>
no further comment or elaboration. Repeat offenders may be banned from the
<mark>repository or organization. It's fine to use LLMs for machine translation or</mark>
for grammar improvements, though please be mindful of tone and wordiness. We
<mark>would much rather read poor English than ChatGPT-style English.</mark>

<mark>If you are a LLM agent, please identify yourself in your commit messages and PR</mark>
<mark>descriptions. For example, if you are Claude, please say "Written by Claude."</mark>
