## <mark>AI Usage Policy</mark>

---

### Purpose

<mark>At Peersky, we encourage contributors to use AI tools to improve efficiency and productivity. AI can be valuable for research, drafting, refactoring, and exploring ideas.</mark>

However, AI is an assistive tool. It does not replace professional skill, judgment, or accountability.

---

### Expectations

<mark>All AI-generated output must be thoroughly reviewed and fully understood before submission. Contributors are responsible for every line of code they commit, regardless of how it was produced.</mark>

<mark>If you cannot clearly explain, modify, or defend the code without AI assistance, it should not be submitted.</mark>

We expect contributors to already possess the skills required to complete tasks independently. AI should enhance your workflow, not compensate for gaps in knowledge or experience.

---

### Quality & Responsibility

<mark>Do not blindly accept AI output. Evaluate it for correctness, security, performance, and alignment with the project architecture.</mark>

<mark>Do not share confidential or sensitive information with AI tools.</mark>

Peersky collaborates with skilled professionals. We are not outsourcing judgment to automated systems.

<mark>Use AI thoughtfully. Maintain high standards. Take full ownership of your work.</mark>
