## <mark>NLnet Generative AI Policy</mark>

<mark>This is an NLnet-funded project. We adhere to the [NLnet Generative AI Policy](https://nlnet.nl/foundation/policies/generativeAI/).</mark>
<mark>If you use generative AI tools like LLMs, code assistants, etc. in your contributions, you must:</mark>
- Disclose any substantive use
- Maintain a prompt provenance log for material contributions
- Ensure outputs can be legally published under FLOS licenses
- <mark>Not present AI-generated content as your own human-authored work</mark>

---

### When it comes to AI - use your best judgment

<mark>I use AI to help plan a strategy and then make changes by hand.</mark>

<mark>What I want to avoid is a situation where copyrighted material from an LLM's training corpus</mark>
makes it into the codebase.

<mark>So please disclose if the _outputs_ of generative AI is what is being committed.</mark>
