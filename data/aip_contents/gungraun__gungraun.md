## <mark>Use of AI tools</mark>

We adhere to the [rust-analyzer] policy:

<mark>AI tool use is not discouraged on the gungraun codebase, as long as it meets our</mark>
<mark>quality standards. We kindly ask you to disclose usage of AI tools in your</mark>
contributions. If you used them without disclosing it, we may reject your
contribution on that basis alone due to the assumption that you have, most
likely, not reviewed your own submission (so why should we?).

<mark>We may still reject AI-assisted contributions if we deem the quality of the</mark>
contribution to be unsatisfactory.
