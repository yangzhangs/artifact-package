## <mark>AI assistance</mark>

> [!IMPORTANT]
>
> <mark>Pierre allows AI-**assisted** _code contributions_, which must be properly</mark>
> disclosed in the pull request.

<mark>If you are using any kind of AI assistance while contributing to Pierre, **this</mark>
<mark>must be disclosed in the pull request**, along with the extent to which AI</mark>
assistance was used (e.g. docs only vs. code generation).

The submitter must have also tested the pull request on all impacted platforms,
<mark>and it's **highly discouraged** to code for an unfamiliar platform with AI</mark>
assistance alone.

> [!WARNING] **Note that AI _assistance_ does not equal AI _generation_**. We
> require a significant amount of human accountability, involvement, and
> <mark>interaction even within AI-assisted contributions. Contributors are required</mark>
> <mark>to be able to understand the AI-assisted output, reason with it, and answer</mark>
> critical questions about it. Should a PR see no visible human accountability
> and involvement, or it is so broken that it requires significant rework to be
> acceptable, **we reserve the right to close it without hesitation**.

<mark>**In addition, we currently restrict AI assistance to code changes only.** No</mark>
<mark>AI-generated media, e.g. artwork, icons, videos and other assets is allowed.</mark>
Likewise, all community interactions, including comments on issues and
discussions and all PR titles and descriptions **must be composed by a human**.
<mark>Community moderators and maintainers reserve the right to mark AI-generated</mark>
responses as spam or disruptive content, and ban users who have been repeatedly
<mark>caught relying entirely on LLMs during interactions.</mark>
