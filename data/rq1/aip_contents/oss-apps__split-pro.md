## Taking issues

<mark>No need to ask for permission when working on the issue, just check out if there is anybody already implementing a solution and submit a PR. Just please keep in mind that reducing the amount of burden you place on maintainers will greatly boost your chances of having it merged. AI slop will not be tolerated, check the AI guidelines below.</mark>

Always feel free to ask questions or seek clarification on the issue. For complex features, it might be best to first agree on an approach with the maintainer.

---

## <mark>AI Contribution policy</mark>

<mark>While I am not against the usage of AI and also utilize it to some extent in this repo, I would like to establish the following policy and AI contributions. Each PR must explicitly</mark>
<mark>state that it was made with the use of AI and to what extent. The changes also MUST be reviewed and I reserve the right to instantly close a PR without comment, as I am not willing to sacrifice</mark>
my time for someone who could not even read and understand what they are submitting.
