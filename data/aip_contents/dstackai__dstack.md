## <mark>AI Assistance Notice</mark>

<mark>If you are using any kind of AI assistance while contributing to `dstack`,</mark>
**this must be disclosed in the pull request**, along with the extent to
<mark>which AI assistance was used.</mark>
As an exception, tab-completions and trivial PRs don't need to be disclosed.

An example disclosure:

> <mark>This PR was written primarily by Claude Code.</mark>

<mark>Failure to disclose this, makes it difficult to determine how much scrutiny to apply to the contribution. Please be respectful to maintainers and disclose AI assistance.</mark>
