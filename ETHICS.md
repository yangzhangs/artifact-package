# Research Ethics

This statement documents the research-ethics procedures for the two online surveys reported in the paper (68 maintainer and 59 contributor responses).

## Institutional review

Our institution does not mandate formal ethics-board approval for this kind of survey study. We nonetheless submitted the study protocol to an internal institutional review, which was granted. As the submission is under double-blind review, we omit institution-specific details here and commit to including them in the camera-ready version.

## Recruitment

- We sampled 1,000 developers (500 maintainers and 500 contributors) from the 18,902 repositories with contribution guidelines in our dataset.
- The surveys were administered as online questionnaires implemented via Google Forms.
- Email addresses were obtained from public GitHub profiles and commit metadata, using only information that the developers made publicly available.
- The invitation described the background and purpose of the study, stated that participation was voluntary, and confirmed that responses would be kept confidential and analyzed in aggregate.

## Data handling

- Recruitment data (the sampled email addresses) were used solely for sending the invitations and were not shared or retained beyond the survey period.
- Responses are released in anonymized per-respondent form, including open-ended comments; names and email addresses are never released.
- The anonymized per-respondent survey responses are provided in `survey/responses/`.
