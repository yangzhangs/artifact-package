## <mark>AI-Generated Code Policy</mark>

<mark>Pull requests that are primarily generated by AI tools (e.g., "vibe coding" or autonomous AI agents) will not be accepted.</mark>

Contributions should be:

- Thoughtfully written by humans
- Well-understood by the contributor
- Properly tested and validated

<mark>Using AI tools to assist with code completion, suggestions, or documentation is fine, but the contributor must understand and take responsibility for all submitted code.</mark>

---

### <mark>Consequences of AI-Generated Code</mark>

We'd rather help you improve a PR than close it. But we can't review work that wasn't reviewed by the person submitting it.

- **First time:** You'll get a warning and a chance to fix the PR.
- **Second time:** The PR will be closed.
- **Repeated offenses:** May be reported to GitHub as spam.
