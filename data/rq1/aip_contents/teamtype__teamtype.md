## <mark>Generative AI/LLM policy</mark>

<mark>If you used LLMs to contribute to this project, we expect you to disclose its usage. This is important for us order for us to figure out with which level of care we should apply when reading the code and providing feedback. Transparency helps us gain trust in the code and in you.</mark>

<mark>You have to take responsibility for your contribution, and make sure that it is high-quality. In particular, you must personally understand and test all output generated by your tool. We like [Zulip's AI use policy and guidelines](https://zulip.readthedocs.io/en/latest/contributing/contributing.html#ai-use-policy-and-guidelines) in this regard.</mark>
