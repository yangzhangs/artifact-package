##### <mark>Do Not Submit AI Generated PRs</mark>

<mark>The same goes for (largely) AI-generated PRs. These are not welcome as they will be based on copyrighted code from others</mark>
without accreditation and without taking the license of the original code into account, let alone getting permission
for the use of the code or for re-licensing.

<mark>Aside from that, the experience is that AI-generated PRs will be incorrect 100% of the time and cost reviewers too much time.</mark>
<mark>Submitting a (largely) AI-generated PR will lead to you being banned from the repo.</mark>
