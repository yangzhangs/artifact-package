## <mark>Restrictions on AI-generated Contributions</mark>

<mark>We DO NOT accept contributions that are generated by AI without human review.</mark>
<mark>The latest LLMs (e.g., OpenAI GPT 5, Google Gemini 2.5 Pro, Anthropic Claude</mark>
Opus 4.1, etc.) often fail to provide reasonable solutions to platform-specific
build issues that arise when mixing different versions of Kotlin, Rust, C/C++,
and Swift. A thorough study of the source code of each platform toolchain is
always needed, where only little related information is available publicly on
the internet.

<mark>Still, using AI on specific tasks, such as generating examples or porting code</mark>
to another langauge, is helpful for productivity. If you have a valid
<mark>justification of your use of LLMs in your contribution, please carefully review</mark>
<mark>the generated content. Sharing the conversation with the LLM might be helpful</mark>
as well.
