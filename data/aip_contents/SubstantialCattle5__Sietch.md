## <mark>AI-Assisted Contributions</mark>

<mark>We recognize that AI tools (GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude, etc.) are increasingly used in software development. We welcome AI-assisted contributions, but with clear expectations around quality and disclosure.</mark>

---

### <mark>Our AI Policy</mark>

---

#### 1. **Disclosure is Required**
- <mark>You **must** disclose if you used AI tools to generate or significantly modify code</mark>
- <mark>Check the "AI assistance" box in the PR template</mark>
- <mark>Briefly describe which parts were AI-assisted and how you validated them</mark>

---

#### 2. **You Are Responsible**
- <mark>AI-generated code must be reviewed, tested, and understood by you</mark>
- You are accountable for all code you submit, regardless of origin
- Ensure the code follows our style guidelines and best practices
- Don't submit code you don't understand

---

#### 3. **Quality Over Quantity**
- <mark>AI-assisted PRs are judged by the same quality standards as any other PR</mark>
- <mark>Bulk AI-generated changes without clear value will be rejected</mark>
- Focus on solving real problems, not just generating code

---

#### 5. **What We Do Accept**
- <mark>✅ Well-tested code that happens to be AI-assisted</mark>
- <mark>✅ AI-generated boilerplate that you've reviewed and customized</mark>
- ✅ Documentation improvements that add real clarity
- ✅ Bug fixes where AI helped you understand the issue
- <mark>✅ Test cases generated by AI that you've validated</mark>

---

### Detection and Labeling

<mark>We use automated workflows to detect likely AI-generated content:</mark>
- <mark>PRs may be automatically labeled `ai-assisted` or `ai-suspected`</mark>
- <mark>This is not a rejection - just a signal for extra human review</mark>
- <mark>If you disclosed AI usage, you'll get the `ai-assisted` label proactively</mark>

---

### <mark>Best Practices for AI-Assisted Contributions</mark>

1. **Start with a real issue**: Identify a genuine problem or enhancement need
2. <mark>**Use AI as a tool, not a shortcut**: Let AI help with boilerplate, but add your expertise</mark>
3. <mark>**Review thoroughly**: Understand every line of AI-generated code</mark>
4. **Test comprehensively**: Write tests or verify existing tests cover your changes
5. **Document your process**: In the PR, explain what you did and why
6. **Engage with reviewers**: Be responsive to feedback and willing to iterate
