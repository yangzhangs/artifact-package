## <mark>On usage of AI and LLMs</mark>

<mark>Usage of AI assistance is usually fine, but you are responsible for making sure the quality of the output is up to the</mark>
<mark>standards of the project. Currently, AI-generated output is often too verbose and its quality is subpar compared to what</mark>
most human contributors produce.

<mark>Do not use an AI or LLM to generate changes, respond to issues or interact with PRs without holding the output to the</mark>
same standards as human-written content.

<mark>Do not submit PRs with descriptions entirely generated by AI. Such PR descriptions are overly verbose and do not</mark>
demonstrate that the human submitter understands the proposed changes.
