### <mark>6. AI-assisted Contributions</mark>
- <mark>Pull requests created with AI assistance are accepted.</mark>
- <mark>AI-generated code must not be low-quality "slop"; it must meet the same quality bar as human-written code.</mark>
- The contributor remains fully responsible for the submission and must understand, review, and refine the generated changes.
- Contributors must run relevant tests and validate the real behavior of the change before opening the pull request.
- If a change is uncertain or requires maintainer-side validation during review, it may be merged temporarily into the `dev` branch first.
- The `main` branch is kept as the clean/stable branch and is used for releases after validation is complete.
